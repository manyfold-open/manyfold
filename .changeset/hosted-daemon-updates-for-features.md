A sandbox or cloud computer whose Manyfold CLI lacks something a turn needs now has its CLI updated on the spot, and the turn goes on once the updated daemon is back, instead of failing as "runner unavailable". The turn asks for a CLI update only when even the latest CLI lacks it or the machine cannot update itself. A computer of your own that lacks it is told to update its CLI, rather than that the runner is unavailable.
