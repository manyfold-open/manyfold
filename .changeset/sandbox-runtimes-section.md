A sandbox's page lists every framework on it under Runtimes, including the CLIs its image ships, each with its agent count and version; the Available frameworks section is gone. A "+" beside Runtimes installs another framework. On every runtime row, and on the mf CLI and herdr controls, clicking the version opens a list to change it, and an arrow beside it leads to the Update Center when a newer one is out. Versions show as the CLI reports them, without an added "v". Detect frameworks moved into the sandbox's "…" menu.
