A machine's status badge now says what its dot shows. A sandbox or cloud computer that is running but whose daemon has not connected reads amber "Not connected" on its page, its Runtimes card, the Cloud computers list and the create form's sandbox cards, instead of a green "Running" beside an amber dot. An agent on such a machine shows "Not connected" rather than "Asleep".
