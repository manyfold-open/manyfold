A sandbox's page has a "?" between its status badge and the refresh button. It opens a panel listing every status the sandbox can show (Running, Suspended, Stopped, Not connected, Deleting, Provisioning, Failed) in its color, with what each one means and whether it counts as running time. The panel opens on a click or tap, and Escape or a click outside closes it.
